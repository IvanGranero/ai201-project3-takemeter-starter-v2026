LOW: CVSS severity LOW; a vulnerability with comparatively limited impact or exploitation conditions.
MEDIUM: CVSS severity MEDIUM; a meaningful risk whose impact or exploitability is constrained.
HIGH: CVSS severity HIGH; a serious vulnerability with substantial security impact.
CRITICAL: CVSS severity CRITICAL; the highest severity category, indicating potentially severe compromise.
